# Delegated CLA acceptance as a boundary case for autonomous OSS-VSM organizations

**Recorded:** 2026-11-13. **Genre:** contemporaneous evidence-linked historiographic note and prospective research protocol. **Status:** `PARENT_DECISION_RECORDED / EXECUTION_UNMEASURED`. **Not:** a legal opinion, retrospective positive autonomy assessment, experimentally established effect or change in normative VSM semantics.

## Research question

Can an organization inspired by the Viable System Model delegate repetitive contributor-license agreement (CLA) acceptance mechanics to AI agents, while keeping legal-person authority, contextual representations and third-party consent observable, bounded and revocable? Which observations would show *operationally exercised delegated authority* rather than merely policy text or possession of a GitHub token?

## Contemporary primary-source chronology

1. **2026-10-26 — new operational environment.** [Organization S5 issue #274](https://github.com/opensiro/vsm-oss-organization/issues/274#issuecomment-6420871211) admitted Upstream Contributions as a fourth S1 domain, where the environment consists of independently governed third-party OSS. The [dated S5 record](../../records/s5/2026-10-26-upstream-domain-admission.md) expressly did *not* confer new external CLA/DCO attestation authority.
2. **2026-11-11 — technical submission separated from consent.** The [Upstream standing publication mandate](https://github.com/opensiro/upstream/blob/main/docs/STANDING_EXTERNAL_PR_PUBLICATION.md) delegated qualifying external PR submission without repeated per-PR human prompts, while excluding fabricated contributor-person legal attestations.
3. **2026-11-11–13 — heterogeneous external restrictions.** [Revka CLA v1.0](https://github.com/KumihoIO/Revka/blob/main/docs/contributing/cla.md) treats opening a contribution as assent (including copyright/patent and rights claims). [Microsoft's Fara #96 policy-service comment](https://github.com/microsoft/fara/pull/96#issuecomment-6810190721) requests an express `agree` message and distinguishes individual and corporate capacity. [VOLY CONTRIBUTING](https://github.com/voly-codes/voly/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) requires DCO `Signed-off-by` on every commit. These requirements are neither semantically nor legally interchangeable.
4. **2026-11-13 — parent authorizes a bounded new class of delegation.** The owner expressly instructed that AI agents should be able to handle CLA acceptance on the owner's behalf **through concrete, organization-specific templates**. The public transcription is [S5 #323](https://github.com/opensiro/vsm-oss-organization/issues/323); the [S5 record](../../records/s5/2026-11-13-cla-template-delegation-decision.md) and [contract](../../contracts/s5/cla-template-delegation.md) separate general authorization from exact-term enrolment, while [Upstream #274](https://github.com/opensiro/upstream/issues/274) owns implementation.

The public issue is a **transcription** of the operator's instruction, not independent evidence of a person's legally effective signature on an external agreement. No third-party CLA acceptance is claimed in this chronology.

## Interpretive VSM analysis

At the declared organizational recursion, selecting **whether and how binding legal representations may be delegated** is an S5 parent-governance matter. Upstream S1 may then execute a sufficiently specified and genuinely authorized template in its external environment, but only within the third party's policy and the real contributor's legal capacity. A credential or an automated GitHub action cannot, by itself, transfer the intellectual-property rights or employer authorization required by the CLA.

Two potential failure mechanisms warrant study:

- **Authority laundering:** equating a GitHub account/bot action, standing PR-publication permission or machine-readable `ACTIVE` flag with informed legal consent from the person or entity whose rights are asserted.
- **Template drift:** silently accepting changed copyright, patent, employer, warranty or company terms after a narrower version was previously approved.

Accordingly, *legitimate authority*, *technical execution*, *target-side acknowledgement* and *observed viable-system closure* are distinct empirical variables. The case does not justify a new S1–S5 definition or an automatic `S1=A`/`S5=A` label.

## Prospective falsifiable study

**Observation unit:** one CLA-bearing proposed contribution with pinned upstream repo/ref, changed files, first-party exact CLA version/digest, approved template ID, actor/capacity, permitted action and target-side receipt.

**H1 (interruptions):** specifically enrolled CLA templates will reduce human-approval interruptions per **eligible** external contribution relative to similar earlier cases.

**H2 (bounded correctness):** a fail-closed template preflight will reject all known out-of-scope actor, employer, terms-drift, revocation and original-repo mismatch test cases; false approvals would falsify the claim.

**H3 (closure):** owner mandate, exact-term enrolment, provider action and third-party confirmation form a reconstructible decision-to-outcome chain. A policy file or GitHub POST alone does not satisfy H3.

**Operational measures:** eligible vs blocked candidate counts; human legal escalations per eligible PR; elapsed time from native-ready patch to external PR; current-vs-pinned CLA-digest mismatches; changed-term blocks; rights/capacity blocks; attempted unsupported acceptances; independent target confirmations; revocation enforcement latency; maintainer outcomes separately. Sample selection, paid prerequisites, missing CI and target-specific rules are confounders.

**Method:** frozen pre-intervention comparison followed by prospective case-level event ledger and independent provider readback. Collect both refusals and permitted acts to avoid counting only successful publications. Preserve version, date, owner, raw policy provenance and fork/base/head identity. No causal gain should be inferred without an adequate comparator.

**Stop/falsification conditions:** an agent accepts terms without exact approval, posts a false person/corporate claim, misses a substantive change to an agreement, acts after revocation, or counts a bot comment as accepted without confirmation. Immediately suspend that path and record incident evidence.
