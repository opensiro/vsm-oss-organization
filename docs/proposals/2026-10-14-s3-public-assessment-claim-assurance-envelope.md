## 1. Specific candidate subject — not a new policy

**Proposed S3 present-control variety:** one **current, materially consequential public commitment to evidence-qualified presentation of OpenSiro assessment claims** across the bounded Index/Skills/Awesome S1 operating system. If a live complementary-audit finding or Index evidence concern materially affects the whole Organization's *present* obligations for a specific publicly represented claim, an authorized S3 holder **might** select a temporary, scoped *operational representation/assurance constraint* on behalf of the whole. The evidenced whole-system control right may affect multiple S1 operations **or** return to one S1 where ordinary local S1/S2 authority demonstrably cannot decide the substantive whole-system matter; multiple affected repositories are not a prerequisite. This concerns *present commitments and constraints*, **not** future methodology, normative VSM semantics or independent grade adjudication.

## 2. Narrow subject-matter eligibility test (all required)

A proposed case is **not S3-eligible** until a source-first read at stated live revisions shows:

1. **One exact public claim and present consequence:** quote or link the precise Index-derived general assessment statement and its current publication/usage; identify what would be materially misleading or incorrectly relied on while a *specific* issue remains unresolved. A mere open correction, audit comment or hypothetical loss of confidence is insufficient.
2. **Whole-system present view:** show real current commitments/constraints and actual dependencies where this exact claim is used by at least one other affected S1 operation, **or** positively demonstrate from pinned primary sources why a material control decision affecting only one S1 genuinely belongs to whole-system S3 rather than that S1's ordinary authority. No separately independent reviewer is universally required by the selected Methodology for a source-first `C` claim. Cross-S1 impact is useful evidence, not a universal S3 requirement.
3. **Residual whole-system discretion:** state the specific choice to temporarily regulate present public *representation/assurance obligations* that the Index correction owner, Skills procedure owner, Awesome local editor, ordinary S2 interference handling or S3* auditor **cannot already fully decide within their authority**. If a local S1 can close it, the case is **NON-ADMIT**.
4. **Legitimate composable S3 decision right:** cite a prospective bounded issuer/right source, candidate owner and affected S1 acknowledgement path. To actually exercise any choice, require separately effective parent-granted authority and a named active holder, as in §§3–6.
5. **Proportional alternatives and testable return:** evidence-supported `CONTINUE` / `SET_CONSTRAINT` / `REQUIRE_REPAIR`, concrete communication effect, expiry, monitoring/return owner and S1 target work reference. No default freeze of a batch, catalog, Index PR, or all public pages.

If any check fails, preserve S1 adjudication/editorial autonomy, record **NOT_ADMITTED** in the owner discussion (not a fabricated S3 transaction) and stop.

## 4. Proposed eligible bounded S3 choices

Only after §§2–3 actually pass, and only for **one named case and current S1 return target**:

| Class | Bounded prospective meaning | Never implies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CONTINUE` | Explicitly preserve the existing current communication/assurance arrangement while the named S1 owner performs ordinary correction review; document material justification. | Endorsing/disproving the disputed vector or waiving review. |
| `SET_CONSTRAINT` | A narrowly attributable, **time-limited claim-presentation qualification** on the specifically evidenced public representation within the adopted Organization envelope; name the exact affected consumer and the allowed wording/status action. | Automatic canonical grade changes, deleting assessment records, global publication hold, revoking Index/Skills/Awesome local rights, forced website changes. |
| `REQUIRE_REPAIR` | Request repair of a **proven cross-boundary claim-communication/assurance mismatch**, within the authorized shared-constraint scope, with the owning S1 choosing the local implementation. | Ordering a particular assessment grade, rewriting pinned evidence, overriding an Index semantic reviewer or ordering methodological changes. |

`STOP`, `SET_RETRY_BUDGET`, `OTHER_CURRENT_CONTROL` are **not granted by this prospective design**. Any expansion needs a new S5 owner decision. Even a permitted class may be rejected/escalated by affected S1 when it exceeds the concrete delegated envelope; S3 may not convert that rejection into implicit authority.

## 5. Hard ownership exclusions

- **Index S1:** sovereign on evidence examination, source pins, acceptance/exclusion, corrections, canonical assessment vectors, adjudication and Index publication gates. This proposal cannot grade Rum, bb, SuisuiCode or impose a general correction queue freeze.
- **Skills S1 / Profile owner:** assessment procedures and normative semantics are untouched. No implicit upgrade of selected Methodology or Profile; no new universal assessment-review gate.
- **Awesome S1:** owns local editorial curation under existing criteria. S3 could regulate an explicitly authorized **whole-system current commitment**, not substitute its editorial judgment or change representative membership by fiat.
- **S3*:** independently scoped complementary evidence/feedback, not decisive S3 control and not an Index GitHub approval.
- **S2:** ordinary inter-S1 interference attenuation without substantive whole-system current regulation does not become S3.
- **S5:** ultimate purpose, policy, delegation-model scope and authority revisions remain with the legitimate parent.
- **Transport:** `TODO.md`, CI, PR merge, templates, issue labels, GitHub permissions and the [offline S3 validator](../../scripts/check_s3_current_control.py) are never independent decisional owners.

Do not silently use private projects or unreleased OpenSiro results. Other repositories' public assessment claims do not automatically enter the bounded Organization recursion.
