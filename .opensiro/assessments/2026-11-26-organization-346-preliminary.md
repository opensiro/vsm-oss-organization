## 2. Selected construction hypothesis and operator outcome

**Target transition:** S4=— → S4=C.

OpenSiro already investigates external organizational models, governance mechanisms and emerging AI-agent capabilities. However, research results do not automatically become adaptation decisions with observable operational closure.

The proposed capability connects:

- external or future-relevant evidence;
- alternative adaptation responses;
- a developer-composed S4 decision owner;
- current S3 capability and constraints;
- authorized S1 implementation;
- subsequent operational feedback.

**Expected benefit — HYPOTHETICAL:** A repeatable path from external architectural evidence to organizational adaptation, without requiring the owner to manually coordinate every intermediate step.

## 5. Proposed acceptance and stop gates

Positive acceptance:

- An external change creates a material adaptation need.
- The composed actor evaluates multiple permissible responses.
- The selected adaptation is checked against current organizational capability.
- An authorized response changes subsequent S1 operation.
- The resulting operational evidence returns into S4 evaluation.

Negative acceptance:

- No adaptation is selected when the external signal is immaterial.
- Missing or revoked authority prevents unauthorized action.
- Reserved identity and policy decisions escalate to S5.
- A research report or backlog update without operational return does not qualify as S4 closure.
- Removing the composed S4 owner eliminates the discretionary adaptation decision.

**Validation:** NOT_RUN.
