## Reserved decisions while parent-governed (S5 P mode)

**Explicit parent clarification:** [Organization #236 owner decision](https://github.com/opensiro/vsm-oss-organization/issues/236#issuecomment-6167754468). While S5 is operated in its **parent-governed mode** (classified as `P` when positively evidenced under the selected Methodology), the legitimate holder named above makes the Organization's **key S5-level decisions**: whether to establish an S3 function for this bounded domain, its mandate and boundaries, S3 delegated authority, holder appointment/succession, activation/withdrawal, and changes to the delegation model. An agent or reviewer can prepare evidence, recommendations and a reviewable implementation, but must not decide any of these unresolved S5 matters on the parent's behalf.

**Separate governance right from evidentiary assessment.** `S5=P` is a *classification of observed parent-governed ownership and closure*, **not the grant of legitimacy**. The current holder's authority comes from this declaration and subsequent attributable parent decisions; it does not automatically disappear or transfer when an assessor changes or cannot establish a label. A transfer or redefinition requires its own legitimate S5 decision. An `INSUFFICIENT` result for a positive `S3=C` evidence claim cannot veto the parent's ability to create **prospective/conditional S3 authority**. Conversely, a parent grant alone does not prove `S3=C`, `S3=A` or a live S3 result.

“Key decisions” means these **reserved S5-level choices**, not ordinary delegated Index/Skills/Awesome S1 review, S2 coordination, S3 present control within an effective grant or S3* audit. A live S3 case still needs its own whole-system functional admission and legitimate S1 return. A prospective appointment or grant may be made **before** any such case, with case conditions to be checked only on exercise. See the [source-pinned incident analysis](../../docs/reviews/2026-10-14-s5-s3-assessment-gate-inversion.md).
