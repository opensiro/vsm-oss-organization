## Actual S3 whole-system regulatory right (within existing S5-issued G1)

S3 regulates **current operational reconciliation of these already-binding Organization-wide commitments when a particular material nonlocal constraint exceeds both local S1 discretion and S2 coordination**, not the canonical fact content or ultimate contract selection. In that restricted domain the legitimate [G1 human S3 holder](parent-assisted-g1.md) may **choose** a narrowly named `CONTINUE`, `REQUIRE_REPAIR` or temporary `SET_CONSTRAINT` *for an admitted real case*, with a source-pinned specific S1 return; that authority is already delegated by [S5 decision #238](https://github.com/opensiro/vsm-oss-organization/issues/238#issuecomment-6169376275). No authority is delegated to the assistant, source collector, deterministic validator or GitHub CI.

This is the functional distinction from the pre-existing constructor template: a bounded **standing current regulation subject** (two current binding Organization-wide commitments) and **specific S3 discretion** over the exceptional reconciliation of **ongoing S1 operations**, as opposed to an abstract schema awaiting an unnamed organizational need. A qualifying choice may affect only one S1, but the regulatory right itself must demonstrably exceed local S1/S2. A single affected admitted S1 work item is allowed **only when independent primary sources from at least two implicated S1 domains** substantiate the current nonlocal constraint. Those source URLs are a necessary screening input, not proof that the issue bodies actually establish the asserted obligation. Not every contract drift, source mismatch, cross-repo dependency, queue size or open issue is such a case.

  --root . --index-root ../vsm-harness-index \

The tests use an expressly *hypothetical* case fixture and do not assert those cited S1 issues are currently admitted together. `--output` writes the observation to a local file; it cannot alter GitHub or S1. A mismatch in existing selected contracts is **not** auto-labelled S3.
